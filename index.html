Computer Systems Technology (Certificate)

Upgrade your career options by learning the fundamentals of computing, PC hardware, and systems configuration. Integrate your software skills with hardware skills and program your path. This certificate opens the door for students to ladder into the Bachelor of Computing Science degree program or pursue careers in programming, network admin, software development, gaming programming, database management, and PC and network support.

Computer Systems Technology (Diploma)

Up your career game in the software development industry by getting creative in our dedicated circuit design and robotics, data communications, and networking labs. Graduates of this program typically forge careers as programmers, analysts, and network administrators in industries that maintain information and telecommunication systems.

Bachelor of Computing Science (Degree)

Tap into your computing science expertise. Explore several areas, including gaming, big data, networking and communications, AI, cloud computing, and mobile applications. A computing science degree opens up opportunities to work in almost any industry that uses computers. Positions in these industries include data scientist, software tester, web developer, systems analyst, business analyst, and software engineer.
